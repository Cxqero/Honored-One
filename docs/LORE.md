# Lore Bible — Limitless & Unlimited Void

Canon-first reference for every move. Anything that deviates from canon must be listed in **§7 Deviations**.

## 0. Source status (as of 2026-09-29)
- **Anime:** Season 1, Season 2 (Hidden Inventory / Shibuya Incident), Season 3 = *Culling Game Part 1* (Jan 9 – Mar 27 2026, 12 eps). *Culling Game Part 2* announced.
- **Shinjuku Showdown (Gojo vs Sukuna, ch. 221–236) is manga-only** → 200% Purple and the remote Purple ("nuke") follow the manga panels.
- **Domain** follows the anime (S1 E7, S2 E9).

## 1. Limitless — the rules
- **Infinity (neutral):** space between Gojo and anything approaching is divided infinitely; it slows to an apparent stop. Always-on for Gojo.
- **Lapse: Blue — 術式順転「蒼」**: amplified cursed energy *converges* space to a point → **field of attraction**. Pulls, crushes, implodes, destroys structures; Gojo also uses it to move/levitate himself (ch. 224).
  - **Maximum Output: Blue** — S2 E3 (Episode 27, vs Toji): clears out the whole immediate area. Ch. 234: collapses Agito into itself, then sends the field at Sukuna. Ch. 230: Blue orbs around opponents, manipulates debris.
  - **Incantation:** 「位相、黄昏、智慧の瞳」 *"Phase, Twilight, Eyes of Wisdom."* (ch. 235, chanted after the fact to boost an existing Blue).
- **Reversal: Red — 術式反転「赫」**: positive energy (reverse cursed technique) through Limitless → **repulsion**. Output at least double Blue's. Small red orb, explosive push.
  - Ch. 231: fired through a building (ricochet ambush) and through the ground.
  - **Incantation:** 「位相、波羅蜜、光の柱」 *"Phase, Paramita, Pillars of Light."* (ch. 233, to restore output).
- **Hollow Technique: Purple — 虚式「茈」**: Blue and Red collide → **imaginary mass** that rushes forth and **erases** whatever it hits ("rips whatever it hits from existence"). Not an explosion.
  - Hand sign (manga): index + pinky extended, then a pinch with index/thumb while extending the middle finger.
  - Uses: vs Hanami (ch. 52 / S1 E20, annihilated a large part of the forest); vs Toji (ch. 75, erased the left side of his body).
  - **Incantation:** 「九綱、偏光、烏と声明、表裏の間」 *"Nine Ropes, Polarized Light, Crow and Declaration, Between Front and Back."*

**VFX language derived from the rules:** Blue pulls debris **in** · Red blasts debris **out** · Purple leaves **nothing** — clean, smooth-edged voids, no rubble.

## 2. 200% Hollow Purple (ch. 223)
- Opening attack of Gojo vs Sukuna.
- Amplification: Utahime's **Solo Forbidden Area** + Gakuganji's performance, synchronized incantations and hand signs (~120%), plus Gojo's own incantation + hand signs → **200%**.
- Fired from **Shibuya**, ripped through numerous streets to Sukuna in **Shinjuku**. Skyscraper crumbled; both of Sukuna's reinforced arms destroyed.
- **In-game:** charge = the Purple incantation (subtitled, line by line) + hand signs; charge meter to 200%; result = very long straight erased trench.

## 3. Remote Hollow Purple — "the nuke" (ch. 234–235)
1. A **Blue** is left floating after Gojo uses Maximum Output Blue to collapse Agito (ch. 234).
2. Gojo fires **Red** separately into the air as a feint.
3. Sukuna tries to force Red to detonate; Gojo **chants Blue's incantation after the fact** to boost Blue's attraction.
4. Gojo chants **Purple's incantation** while he, Sukuna and Mahoraga are all airborne; Red and Blue **converge** → imaginary mass **decimates a large stretch of Shinjuku**.
5. Mahoraga and its wheel completely disintegrated. Sukuna survives, heavily injured, missing his left hand.
6. **Gojo was inside the blast, without Infinity, and took only minimal damage** (his own cursed energy).
- **In-game:** Blue + Red placed on either side of the target in the sky, caster airborne, converge → massive erasure. **Caster takes minimal self-damage** (configurable). Matches Cxqero's reference clip (see `storyboard/NUKE_STORYBOARD.md`).

## 4. Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void — 無量空処 (Muryōkūsho)
- **Hand sign:** index finger wrapped around the middle finger (Taishakuten seal / 帝釈天印).
- **Barrier** closes around the area; interior is "the inside of the Unlimited" — a universe-like space. Anime (S1 E7) gives it an extended formation sequence; manga is near-instant.
- **Sure-hit effect:** targets receive boundless information — *"I can see everything! I can feel everything! The information has no end!"* — and cannot act. Gojo then finishes them himself (anime: decapitates Jogo).
- **Exclusion:** the effect does not apply to the user or anyone **touching him with no intent to harm** (Itadori, S1 E7).
- **vs Sukuna (manga):** expanded it five times; each time his burnt-out technique was repaired with reverse cursed technique; compressed the barrier from Sukuna-range to "the size of a sports ball" (ch. 227). → canon basis for no cooldowns.

### 4a. The 0.2-second domain (S2 E9 / Episode 33; Shibuya B5F)
- Opened for **0.2 s** — Gojo's estimate of the maximum a non-sorcerer could endure without lasting damage.
- ≈ **half a year's worth of information** forced into everyone in range.
- **Everyone was knocked out while still standing.** Non-sorcerers needed ~**two months** to rejoin society; the special-grade curses "could awaken at any moment."
- Gojo then eliminated ~1,000 transfigured humans in **299 seconds**.
- **Canon has no buffs for Gojo.** The payoff is the window of total paralysis.

## 6. Canon-driven design additions (proposed)
- Kanji titles on cast: 蒼 / 赫 / 茈 / 無量空処. Incantation subtitles (JP + EN) during charges. Text only, no voice.
- Domain exclusion: tamed pets / villagers adjacent to the player are spared.
- 0.2s domain: passive mobs & villagers ("non-sorcerers") knocked out unharmed; bosses recover sooner (special grades).
- Optional **Infinity** passive (decision pending).

## 7. Deviations from canon (must stay listed)
- The player's skin replaces Gojo (no character model).
- Minecraft-scale tuning of radii/durations (all configurable).
- **0.2s domain buffs** — canon effect (paralysis of everyone in range) is kept; caster buffs are added on top. Cxqero's decision, 2026-09-29.
- **Solo 200% Purple** — canon required Utahime + Gakuganji; in-mod the full incantation + hand signs alone reach 200%. Cxqero's decision, 2026-09-29.
- **Full domain caster buffs** — the same configurable buffs as the 0.2 s domain while the full domain is up (canon: the sure-hit is the whole payoff). Session 2.
- **The void's interior hides the world** — in S1E7 the victims float in the void; in-game the terrain inside the barrier is hidden for anyone inside (client-side, toggle `domainHideTerrain`); the ground still holds everyone up.
- **Red's pressure and the domain's expansion flatten tall grass and flowers** around the caster (cosmetic, only with destruction on) so the close shots aren't blocked by plants.
- **The nuke's bloom is shown from in front of Gojo** (the clip frames the mass erupting behind his face; physically it erupts ahead of him).
