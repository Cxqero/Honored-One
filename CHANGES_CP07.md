# Checkpoint #7 — what was done and added (2026-09-30)

## Domain Expansion: new sound design
Every domain sound was redesigned and is now **stereo**. Minecraft plays stereo sounds around you instead of at a
point in the world, so inside the domain it surrounds you. Each cue is timed to the new, longer build-up.

| Sound | What you hear |
|---|---|
| Seal | A cloth sweep and a dry tick as the seal sets. The Six Eyes open in a crystalline shimmer that climbs while a low pressure gathers. The world breathes in. |
| White | Everything drops out: a thin trembling ring, a distant breath of choir, pressure in your ears, then a sharp inhale. |
| Ink | A deep, wet eruption with droplets scattering. Then space manifests: neon lines zip into being faster and faster, crackling, over a chord swelling out of the dark. |
| Tunnel | An endless rising Shepard glissando that keeps accelerating, rushing air, chattering data, a quickening heartbeat, all cut dead at the flash. |
| Open | A vast struck bell over a floor dropping away, with crystal overtones. The reverb opens into infinite space. |
| Void | A seamless loop: a deep breathing chord, cosmic wind moving across you, distant stars ringing, and something vast singing once, far away. |
| Collapse | The void is inhaled, cracks and shatters like glass, the ink splashes, and the world rushes back. |
| Instant | The 0.2-second domain with every beat in place: snap, ring, ink, bell, wipe. |

## Domain Expansion: cast in mid-air
If you cast the domain in mid-air, an **invisible platform** forms under you across the whole domain, so no one
inside falls through. It can't be seen, targeted or broken. It disappears when the domain ends or is cancelled.
If the world closes mid-domain, any leftover floor removes itself. Tested: you land on it and stay there, and when
the domain ends nothing is left behind.

## Maximum Output: Blue
- **No more arm through the body.** Your raised right hand conducts Blue from overhead as it orbits, tracing a
  circle above your head. Your torso turns and leans with it, and your legs walk normally if you move while
  charging.
- **Letting go:** you raise Blue above your head, hold it there a beat, and it **disperses**. It still collapses
  and explodes, then streams of blue light spread and thin away while glittering motes drift and sink. It's no
  longer thrown like a mini Hollow Purple.
- The throw and collapse-in-place options are still there in the settings, under "Maximum Output: what happens
  when you let go". The setting was renamed so the new behaviour replaces the old saved default.

## Hollow Purple (the nuke): bigger explosion, and a shockwave
- **Fireball:** bigger. It cools faster from white-hot into dark violet smoke with embers inside, with a lumpy,
  rolling outline instead of a round ball.
- **Shockwave:** a shock front races out along the ground to about six times the blast radius, fast at first and
  then slowing. It carries a tall two-tier wall of dust, a condensation shell through the air and a pressure
  flash on the ground.
- **When the front reaches you:** your camera jolts, the screen flashes briefly and a new shockwave roar plays.
- **Gameplay:** mobs and other players in its path are thrown outward and hurt; you ride it out.
- **Clearer detonation:** the magenta screen grade clears at detonation so the explosion shows its own colours,
  and the detonation flash is shorter.
