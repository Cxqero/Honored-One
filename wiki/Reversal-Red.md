> **術式反転「赫」 Cursed Technique Reversal: Red.** Positive energy run through Limitless, Blue reversed: a repulsion
> at least twice its output.

<p align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/igktl0L.gif" width="720"></p>

## Casting

| Input | Technique |
|---|---|
| **Tap `G`** | **Reversal: Red**: Red ignites at your fingertip and fires |
| **Hold `G`** | **Incantation Red**: chant, then release the full-power shot |

## Reversal: Red (tap)
- Red ignites at your fingertip and fires at 75 blocks per second
- Where it strikes, everything is repelled outward: creatures thrown, debris blasted away, dust billowing up
- Your arm kicks back with the recoil

## Incantation Red (hold)
Hold `G` and chant, word by word, with laser rays lancing out and the edges of the screen bleeding red:

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 位相 | 波羅蜜 | 光の柱 |
| *Phase* | *Paramita* | *Pillars of Light* |

Release and Red fires as a **drilling shot**: it bores through up to **70 blocks** of terrain, then detonates in a
blast nearly three times the size, with an impact frame on the hit.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{REPO}}/main/wiki/images/shot_red_rays.jpg" width="720"></p>

## Defaults

| | Tap | Incantation |
|---|---|---|
| Range | 110 blocks | 240 blocks |
| Speed | 75 blocks/s | 120 blocks/s |
| Blast radius | 6.5 blocks | 17 blocks |
| Damage | 34 | 130 |
| Knockback | 3.4 | 7.5 |
| Chant time | — | 3 seconds |

Everything is adjustable under **Settings → Reversal: Red**.
