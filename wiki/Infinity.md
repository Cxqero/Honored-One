> **無下限呪術 Limitless, neutral.** The space between you and anything approaching is divided infinitely. It slows,
> and never arrives.

## How it works
- **On by default** whenever you join a world. Press **`N`** to toggle it
- Arrows and other projectiles slow down from 4.5 blocks out and **stop** 1.2 blocks from you, hanging in the air
- **Melee hits and mob contact** are blocked
- **Explosion damage** is blocked
- A faint ripple shows where something was stopped
- **Fall damage still applies**: canon Infinity doesn't stop the ground (you can change that)

## Settings
| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| On when you join | on |
| Slow-down distance | 4.5 blocks |
| Stop distance | 1.2 blocks |
| Block melee / projectiles / explosions | on / on / on |
| Block fall damage | off |
| Ripples | on |
