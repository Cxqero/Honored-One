## Casting

| Input | Technique |
|---|---|
| **Tap `Z`** | **The 0.2-second domain** (Shibuya): it opens for an instant |
| **Hold `Z`** | **The full domain** (S1 E7): the whole expansion |
| **Press `Z` again** | collapse your domain early, wiped away by white ink |

## The full domain (hold)
About six seconds of expansion, after the anime:
1. Six Eyes close-up
2. The seal: index finger wrapped around the middle finger, on your blocky fingers
3. The world turns white, with a pink cast on everyone in it
4. Ink bursts in, and neon lines draw themselves out of it, faster and faster
5. The **tunnel of information**, a heartbeat speeding up, then the flash (an impact frame)
6. **The void**

## Inside the void
- The world disappears: terrain, sky, clouds and weather. Everyone floats in space
- A 360° nebula, the giant **black hole** with its ring turning and smoke pouring off it, and wisps drifting around you,
  all made in Blender
- **Sure-hit:** everyone caught can't move, turn, attack or use items, and their screen floods with information
- **You** get Strength II, Speed I and Resistance I while it's up
- Anyone **touching you** when it expands is left out (canon: Gojo holding Itadori)
- Bosses (the Wither, the Ender Dragon, Wardens) shake it off three times faster
- Cast it in mid-air and an invisible floor forms so nobody falls through. It removes itself afterwards

## The 0.2-second domain (tap)
Snap, ring, ink, a bell, a wipe: the domain is up for an instant and **everyone in range is knocked out standing** for
10 seconds. You keep your buffs for 15 seconds.

## Defaults

| Setting | Full | 0.2 s |
|---|---|---|
| Radius | 32 blocks | 24 blocks |
| Duration | 30 s (or until you collapse it) | an instant |
| Paralysis | while inside, plus 3 s | 10 s |
| Cooldown | none | none |

Choose who gets caught (players, passive mobs, hostile mobs), your buffs, rooting and a cooldown under
**Settings → Domain Expansion**. Visual options (hide the world, the black hole, the wisps, the void's brightness, the
barrier) are under **Client**.
