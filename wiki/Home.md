## 天上天下唯我独尊

**Honored One** brings Satoru Gojo's **Limitless** into Minecraft 1.21.1: every technique from Lapse: Blue to the
remote Hollow Purple that levelled Shinjuku, with Blender-made effects, in-engine cinematics, Shinjuku-arc sound design
and full shader-pack support. **You** are the sorcerer: your own character and skin cast every technique.

> Pull enemies into **Blue**, send them flying with **Red**, erase a path through the landscape with **Hollow Purple**,
> drop the sky on them with the **nuke**, and trap everyone in **Unlimited Void**.

- **Cinematic cutscenes** authored in Blender and played live in your world: skip any of them with `Enter`
- **Full-screen Blender inserts** at 1080p for the nuke's collision, implosion and star glint
- **Blocky fingers** for every hand sign, in third person, close-ups and first person
- **Six Eyes**: your skin's eyes blaze cyan during the big techniques
- **Anime impact frames** and **speed lines** on the heaviest moments
- **Body physics** on every casting animation: follow-through, overshoot and recoil
- **Shinjuku-arc sound design**: 43 original sounds, chest-hitting sub-bass, cracking space, a dark choir
- **Shader packs**: energy effects are drawn on top of the pack's image, so Bliss, Complementary and friends can't break them
