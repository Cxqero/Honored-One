> **虚式「茈」 Hollow Technique: Purple.** Blue and Red collide to create an imaginary mass that rushes forth and erases
> whatever it hits. Not an explosion: whatever it touches simply stops existing.

## Casting

| Input | Technique |
|---|---|
| **Tap `V`** | **Hollow Purple**: Red and Blue meet between your hands and the mass launches |
| **Hold `V`** | **200% Hollow Purple**: the full incantation with hand signs, then the release |

## Hollow Purple (tap)
- A short cutscene: Red and Blue form at your hands and collide
- The imaginary mass rushes forward up to 140 blocks, **erasing** everything in a 4.5-block radius: clean, smooth-edged
  space with no rubble
- It explodes where it first strikes (a flash, a purple fireball, a shock dome, a ring of dust) and keeps going

## 200% Hollow Purple (hold)
Hold `V` through the full four-line incantation, each line with its own hand sign, while the charge climbs to 200% and
the screen turns violet:

| | | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 九綱 | 偏光 | 烏と声明 | 表裏の間 |
| *Nine Ropes* | *Polarized Light* | *Crow and Declaration* | *Between Front and Back* |

Release and a cinematic plays: the camera sweeps around you as Red and Blue collide into a **deep violet mass** with a
burning rim and lightning crawling across it. It launches with an impact frame, speed lines and a camera punch, and
carves a straight **420-block erased trench**.

## Defaults

| | Tap | 200% |
|---|---|---|
| Radius of the mass | 4.5 blocks | 11 blocks |
| Range | 140 blocks | 420 blocks |
| Speed | 70 blocks/s | 110 blocks/s |
| Damage | 120 | 1000 |
| Chant time | — | 4 seconds |

> ⚠️ **200% Purple is enormous.** For a more lore-friendly playthrough, lower its range and radius in the settings.

<details>
<summary><b>Canon notes</b></summary>

- Hollow Purple annihilated a large part of the forest vs Hanami (S1 E20) and erased the left side of Toji's body
  (ch. 75).
- **200%** opens the Shinjuku Showdown (ch. 223): with Utahime's Solo Forbidden Area and Gakuganji's performance it is
  fired from Shibuya, rips through the streets to Sukuna in Shinjuku and destroys both of his reinforced arms.
- In this mod you reach 200% alone with the full incantation and hand signs. This is a deliberate change from canon.
</details>
