> **術式順転「蒼」 Cursed Technique Lapse: Blue.** Amplified cursed energy converges space to a single point, and
> everything is pulled toward it.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{REPO}}/main/wiki/images/gifs/blue.gif" width="720"></p>

## Casting

| Input | Technique |
|---|---|
| **Tap `R`** | **Lapse: Blue**: a singularity opens where you aim |
| **Hold `R`** | **Maximum Output: Blue**: Blue orbits you and grows for as long as you hold |

## Lapse: Blue (tap)
- A singularity opens at your crosshair (up to 48 blocks away)
- Creatures and items are dragged into it, and anything caught in the core is crushed
- It tears the terrain around it into flying chunks and swallows them
- After a few seconds it collapses with a crushing blast and leaves a crater behind

## Maximum Output: Blue (hold)
- Blue forms beside you and swells for as long as you hold, from a small orb to a 9-block-radius sphere over 6 seconds
- It orbits you, your raised hand conducting it overhead, ripping up everything it passes through
- Let go and it is raised above your head, held for a beat, and **disperses**: it collapses, then streams of blue light
  spread and fade while glittering motes drift down
- Prefer the old way? Settings → *Maximum Output: what happens when you let go*: **throw** it or **collapse in place**

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{REPO}}/main/wiki/images/shot_blue_orbit.jpg" width="720"></p>

## Defaults

| | Tap | Maximum Output |
|---|---|---|
| Range / orbit | 48 blocks | orbits up to 15 blocks out |
| Size | core 2.1, pull field 16 blocks | 1.4 → 9 blocks over 6 s |
| Damage | 24/s in the core, 30 on collapse | 60/s inside, 80 on collapse |
| Terrain | 5.5-block crater | tears 1.25× its size |

Everything is adjustable under **Settings → Lapse: Blue**.

  Agito into itself and orbits Sukuna, dragging debris with it (ch. 230, 234).
- Blue has an incantation, "Phase, Twilight, Eyes of Wisdom", but Gojo only chants it once, after the fact, to boost a
  Blue that already exists. In this mod that happens in the [[Remote Hollow Purple|Remote-Hollow-Purple]].
</details>
