> **The Hollow Purple nuke** (ch. 234–235). A Blue left floating, a Red thrown into the sky as a feint, both chanted
> back to full power, and the two brought together over the target. It levelled a stretch of Shinjuku.

## Casting
Aim at a creature (or the ground) between **24 and 96 blocks** away and **tap `B`**. Your other techniques are locked
until it ends. Press `Enter` to skip the cinematic.

## The cinematic (~29 seconds)
1. **Red** forms at your raised fingertip with rays lancing out, and you chant *Phase, Paramita, Pillars of Light*
2. The two-finger sign in close-up, then you hurl Red into the sky (speed lines) and the camera follows it up
3. **Six Eyes** close-up: your eyes blaze cyan
4. **Blue** far across the sky, boosted by its incantation, *Phase, Twilight, Eyes of Wisdom*: a cyan beam and
   spiralling arms wind into it
5. Blue's pull tears your target and everything near it off the ground; you **leap** after them (speed lines)
6. In mid-air, the Purple incantation, *Nine Ropes, Polarized Light, Crow and Declaration, Between Front and Back*,
   while Red and Blue rush in with lightning between them
7. **Collision** (impact frames), a silent **implosion**, and the imaginary mass **blooms**: a white heart, the whole sky
   magenta, your silhouette against it
8. The **star glint**, then the **detonation**: a fireball that cools into dark violet smoke, a mushroom column, and a
   **shockwave** racing across the land to six times the blast radius
9. You drift down into a **~120-block crater** with slow falling

## Gameplay
- Everything inside the erasure takes 5000 damage; items and projectiles vanish
- **You take only minimal damage** (4 by default) and it is never lethal (canon: Gojo was caught in the blast without
  Infinity)
- Creatures in the shockwave's path are thrown outward and hurt
- The crater is carved with its own block budget so it keeps pace with the visuals

## Defaults

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Target distance | 24–96 blocks |
| Lift height / lift radius | 18 / 9 blocks, up to 12 creatures |
| Erasure radius | 64 blocks (a ~120-block crater) |
| Time to full size | 3 seconds |
| Damage / self-damage | 5000 / 4 |
| Slow falling afterwards | 14 seconds |

> ⚠️ **This is the biggest thing in the mod.** Lower the erasure radius in **Settings → Remote Hollow Purple** if you play
> near anything you want to keep.
